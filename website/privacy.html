Privacy Policy

This page explains the data handled by this Crowdmap deployment when you browse maps, submit reports, or administer map changes.

Last updated: 5 September 2026

Reporter pseudonyms and map reports

Each map report includes a reporter name. Choose a pseudonym rather than a real name, and do not include personal information in it. You may change or rotate your pseudonym at any time.

The pseudonym is used only to count distinct reporters who have seen the same change. That count helps map users and reviewers decide when a report has enough independent confirmation to include in a map. Pseudonyms attached to reports are stored with those reports; changing to a new pseudonym does not rename or remove earlier reports.

A report also stores its map-change details, such as room or area identifiers, names, coordinates, exits, and any submitted map data. Do not place personal information in report fields. Reports remain available until they are incorporated into the baseline map or removed by an authorised administrator.

Technical and security logs

The service may log your IP address for debugging, reliability, and security purposes. Request logs also include a request ID, request method and path, response status, and processing duration. Logs are retained according to the policy of the operator running this deployment and are not used for advertising or profiling.

Browser storage and cookies

The explorer stores your selected report-count threshold in this browser’s local storage so the setting persists between visits. The review page keeps an administrator API key only in page memory while it is open; it does not save that key in browser storage. This service does not set its own tracking or advertising cookies.

Access and sharing

Report data is used to operate, review, and publish the crowdmap. Authorised map administrators can access pending report details while reviewing changes. Service operators and their hosting, database, and backup providers may process the data as needed to run and secure the deployment. We do not sell personal data.

Your choices and contact

You can use a new pseudonym for future reports and can clear the local storage setting through your browser. To ask about a report, logs, or deletion, contact the operator of the specific Crowdmap deployment. Self-hosted deployments may have their own contact details, retention periods, and additional privacy terms.
