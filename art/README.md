# CrowdMap Visual Emblem Concept

The emblem represents CrowdMap as a shared model of a fictional world built from many individual observations.

## Core concept

The large graph in the lower half represents the consolidated MUD map:

- rooms are nodes
- exits are connections
- the structure is intentionally abstract rather than geographic

This reflects how MUD maps are fundamentally graph structures rather than conventional street maps.

## Reports and observations

The smaller cloud-like subgraphs above represent individual reports or local views of that same world.

Their cloud/thought-like shapes suggest:

- “my observation”
- “my view of the world”
- a local perspective rather than a separate map or document

Each report shows mostly familiar local structure, with one emphasized difference such as:

- an added room or exit
- a changed connection
- a missing connection
- a confirming observation

Faded and dotted elements provide context without competing with the reported change.

## Consolidation

Together, the composition expresses the core idea of the service:

> **Multiple partial and sometimes differing observations are collected and consolidated into one shared map graph.**

## Fantasy and exploration context

The compass frame and restrained fantasy-cartography styling establish the fictional-world and exploration context without turning the logo into a conventional geographic-map symbol.

The dark teal, parchment, and gold palette supports that fantasy identity while keeping the graph itself visually dominant.

## Key concepts

- shared graph
- local observations
- corroboration
- differing reports
- consolidation
- fictional-world mapping
- exploration

## Guidance for derived assets

When creating additional assets from the master emblem:

- preserve the core composition and semantics
- simplify rather than redesign
- keep the graph as the primary visual element
- retain the idea of cloud-like local report views
- avoid introducing GPS/map-pin imagery
- avoid making the reports look like documents, rain, data streams, or magical particles
- preserve the fantasy-cartography character in a restrained way
